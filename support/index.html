Bug reports, dead stream links, missing shows, or just to say hi.

A song won't play

Recordings stream live from the Internet Archive, so you need an internet connection. If one particular take fails, try another version from the song's takes tab — the Archive occasionally moves or removes a file. Tell me which show and I'll fix the link in the next update.

Vibe search isn't finding what I mean

Search matches on how the music sounds, not on titles or lyrics. Describe texture, tempo and mood — "slow heavy doom", "bright jangly boogie" — rather than song names. It runs entirely on your phone, so it works offline.

A show is missing

The catalog is built from the band's shows on the Internet Archive and updated with app releases. If a show you love isn't in there, send me the date and venue.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 17 or later
  • Internet connection for audio streaming (search and browsing work offline)
