Privacy policy

No data is collected. No tracking. Ever.

That's the whole policy. The details below spell it out.

What Gizzverse collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no identifiers of any kind. It does not know who you are.

What stays on your phone

Your favorites are saved only on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests the app makes

  • Audio streaming from the Internet Archive (archive.org) when you play a recording.
  • Setlist and song information from kglw.net when you open a song or show page.

Those services see the same standard request any web browser would make — your IP address and the file requested. Gizzverse adds nothing to identify you. Vibe search runs entirely on your phone and makes no network request.

Children

Gizzverse does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with the date updated below.
